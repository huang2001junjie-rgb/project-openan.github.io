China Unicom Joins the OpenAN Open-Source Community

News · October 14, 2026 · by OpenAN

Announcement Community China Unicom

The OpenAN community welcomes China Unicom as a new member of the OpenAN open-source project. China Unicom, a major Chinese telecommunications operator, has joined the project, expanding industry participation in the community's ongoing work on open autonomous networking. The move strengthens collaboration among project participants and marks another step in the growth of the OpenAN ecosystem. Readers can follow the GitHub repository and the project website for the latest updates.
